## The three big stories

1. **Cyclone Akvan slams India's east coast** — Landfall near Gopalpur
   around 07:30–08:30 IST, winds 70–80 km/h gusting 90. More than
   300,000 people evacuated to 9,341 shelters; no deaths reported as the
   storm weakened. Third cyclone to threaten Odisha in 24 days.
   (IMD bulletins, Reuters)
2. **Russian missiles hit three Kyiv bridges; three killed** — Overnight
   barrage hits the Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi
   bridges and knocks out power, heat and water. A day after strikes
   damaged the Dutch and Austrian embassies, the EU summons Russia's
   envoy. (Reuters)
3. **COP31 reaches its final day with fossil fuels erased from the
   draft** — Thursday's text cut from 144 to 80 paragraphs, every
   fossil-fuel reference and the $120bn figure gone. The "35 by 35"
   clean-power goal is backed by about 75 nations; the final text is due
   at midnight, with overtime expected. (UN News, CNN)
